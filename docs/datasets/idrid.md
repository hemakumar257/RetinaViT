# IDRiD (Indian Diabetic Retinopathy Image Dataset)

## Source
- **Website**: [IDRiD Grand Challenge](https://idrid.grand-challenge.org/)

## Instructions
1. **Registration**: Register on the IDRiD Grand Challenge website or IEEE Dataport.
2. **Download**: Download the "A. Segmentation" and "B. Grading" subsets.
3. **Placement**: Unzip into `datasets/raw/idrid/`.

## Expected Structure
```text
datasets/raw/idrid/
├── A. Segmentation/
│   ├── 1. Original Images/
│   └── 2. All Segmentation Groundtruths/
└── B. Grading/
    ├── 1. Original Images/
    └── 2. Groundtruths/
```
